    Questions

    What do you clean?

    Apartments and condo units: move-in/move-out, deep cleaning and bi-weekly upkeep. We don't take large homes or commercial spaces.

    Does the apartment need to be empty for a move-out clean?

    Yes. Move-in/move-out cleaning is for vacant units, and utilities need to be on at the time of service.

    Is the price on this page final?

    It's an estimate. Your final price is confirmed after a quick walkthrough or photos. Extra time for heavy buildup is $75 an hour.

    How does bi-weekly cleaning work?

    It's a contract with a 3-month minimum, every two weeks. We start with a one-time deep clean. Spots are limited, so we confirm bi-weekly requests before booking.
